# Behavioral

Prepare behavioral rounds from your own story bank, which stays outside this repository:

- Write your stories by theme (conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, impact), each in a situation, task, action and result shape.
- Map common questions to the story that answers them best.
- Keep the story bank with your other study guides, under your [study-guides root](../../docs/specs/content.md#study-guides).

This track has no items, notes or scheduling, so `cstudy next --track behavioral` reports nothing in scope. No story-bank content belongs in this repository ([content](../../docs/specs/content.md#track-notes)).
